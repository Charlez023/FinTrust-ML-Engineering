# FinTrust ML Engineering — Success Criteria

Week 1 will be considered successful if the main MLE requirements and plans are clearly understood and documented.

**Business:** The role of MLE in the FinTrust solution is clearly defined.

**Data:** The customer and transaction datasets, including their relationship through `Customer_ID`, are understood.

**Validation:** The required data-quality and schema checks are identified.

**Preprocessing and Features:** A reusable approach for preparing the data and creating model features is defined.

**Model and Inference:** The planned connection between prepared features, the trained model, and prediction output is documented.

**Testing:** Unit, integration, and end-to-end testing requirements are identified.

**Repository and Dependencies:** The project structure is organised and dependencies on other project tracks are documented.

**Risks and Reproducibility:** Key technical risks are identified, and the project structure and dependencies are documented well enough for future implementation.
